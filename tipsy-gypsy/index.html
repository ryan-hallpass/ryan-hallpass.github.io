What You're Building
Three businesses, one building,
one opportunity.

A downtown bar opening at the end of May. A boutique at street level. Six short-term rental units upstairs, already trading on platforms but without a brand, a website, or a connective story. Each piece has demand. But the real unlock is when they operate as one experience — a guest who books the Whistlestop walks downstairs into the Tipsy Gypsy, and a visitor drinking at the bar leaves with a reason to come back and stay the night.

This proposal builds the brand, the systems, and the cross-promotion engine that turns three overlapping businesses into one hospitality destination — and opens the doors with a presence that matches what Lance is actually building.

Where Things Stand
The opportunity is real.
The infrastructure isn't built yet.
  • No consolidated brand — the bar, the boutique, and the upstairs rentals need a shared visual identity and a story that ties them together.
  • No website — nowhere to send traffic, book direct, capture leads, or tell the full story of what's happening downtown.
  • Listings are live but not optimized — Airbnb and VRBO copy is functional, not converting. No Google Business Profile for either business.
  • No photography system — professional photos are the single biggest lever for STR conversion rates and they don't exist yet.
  • No CRM or automations — every guest leaves without a path back, and every bar patron is a lead that gets lost.
  • Cross-promotion between the units, the bar, and the boutique isn't wired up — which is where the real margin lives.
