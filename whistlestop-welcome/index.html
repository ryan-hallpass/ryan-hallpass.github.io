Chapter 01
Where to eat.
01

Downtown Ardmore punches way above its weight. Four favorites — all a short walk from where you're staying.

Downtown · Dinner hour
Cafe Alley
2 min walk

Chef-driven American plates in a restored downtown storefront. The date-night move.

Casa Romo
3 min walk

Tex-Mex with a patio, heavy pours, and the kind of queso that ruins all other queso.

Hamburger Inn
4 min walk

Open since the 1930s. Order a cheeseburger, fries, and a chocolate malt. Trust.

Ten Star Pizza
5 min walk

Wood-fired pies and local beer. Noisy, crowded, and exactly right on a Friday night.
