Where to eat.

Four favorites — all a short walk
Cafe Alley
6 min walk →

Chef-driven American plates in a restored downtown storefront. The date-night move.

3 min walk →

Tex-Mex with a patio, heavy pours, and the kind of queso that ruins all other queso.

2 min walk →

Open since the 1930s. Order a cheeseburger, fries, and a chocolate malt. Trust.
