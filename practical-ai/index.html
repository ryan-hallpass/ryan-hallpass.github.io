One distinction that explains everything

The intern vs. the employee.

ChatGPT, Claude — “the intern”

You ask.
It answers.
You ask again.

Brilliant — but it only moves when you push it. Every step is on you.

You are the engine.
An agent — “the employee”

You give it a goal.
It figures out
the steps.

Starts out green — then gets sharper as it learns your business, like any new hire.

It is the engine.
