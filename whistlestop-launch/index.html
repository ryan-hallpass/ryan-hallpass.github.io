Whistlestop’s Booking Engine
A guide to platforms
actually built for short-term rentals.

Here’s the platform landscape for the Whistlestop’s booking system, plus why we’re steering away from Wix. Booking platform setup and day-to-day operations stay with you (or a dedicated STR co-host) — Hallpass focuses on the marketing site and integrates whichever platform you choose into the Whistlestop website build.

A note on Wix

Wix Bookings is built for service appointments — haircuts, classes, massages — not multi-night stays. The original Wix Hotels product was deprecated; current STR functionality on Wix is third-party integrations bolted onto a builder that wasn’t designed for this category.

Wix doesn’t handle nightly pricing logic, minimum-stay rules, channel sync to Airbnb / Vrbo / Booking.com, or security-deposit holds — all table-stakes for an STR. If Whistlestop ever lists on Airbnb, a Wix-only setup creates real double-booking risk.

After evaluating the major STR platforms, here’s the one we recommend for the 6-unit Whistlestop, assuming you want direct bookings (not just OTA distribution).

Hospitable Professional →

  • ·Channel sync — Airbnb, Vrbo, Booking.com, Google Vacation Rentals all kept in lockstep automatically
  • ·$5M damage protection on direct bookings — risk coverage that competing platforms charge extra for or don’t offer

We considered the alternatives. Some competitors are cheaper or charge no transaction fees, but they fall short on integration polish with a custom-built site. For the Whistlestop’s scope and your team’s daily experience, the trade-off lands in Hospitable’s favor.
