04 — Self-service

Members edit it themselves

One private link. No login. No bottleneck.

  • Each business gets a private edit link — one click opens their page in an editor.
  • They control headline, story, photos, hours, contact, and the call-to-action — with a live preview.
  • Edits go live instantly; the Chamber never has to be the middleman.
  • Distributed through a Chamber-controlled console — you decide who gets their link, when.

07 — The model

Why a share, not a flat fee: it aligns the incentive. When the Chamber promotes premium upgrades to its members, the Chamber earns alongside us — a recurring, non-dues revenue stream tied directly to member value.

This is an opening figure to pressure-test together, not a fixed ask. Free tier (the business's own brand + content) stays free.
