Hallpass × Ardmore Chamber 08 — How we drive traffic

Ways we bring traffic to the Member Spotlights and the Directory.

Play 01 — Chamber social

Share the Business Spotlight.

When you post about a member, link their Spotlight instead of a plain photo. Same effort, and the post sends real traffic to a page with their hours, photos, reviews, and a link to their site.

Play 02 — Editorial content

Content that doesn't read like the Chamber wrote it.

Guides people search for — Best Local Restaurants in Ardmore, 8 Best Shops in Downtown Ardmore — that stand on their own and link into member Spotlights. The first guides are live.

ardmore.townspark.app

Hallpass writes and hosts the editorial. It uses a separate voice from the Chamber's on purpose — a visitor trusts a "best of" guide differently than a membership page.

Member publicity report
