08 — The bigger idea

The bigger idea

An internal tool helps members. A destination brings them customers.

Right now the directory is something the Chamber points members to. The asset gets meaningfully more valuable when people outside the Chamber land on it — someone searching for a plumber, a dentist, a place to eat.

That's the difference between a member benefit and a member lead source — and it's what makes a paid upgrade worth buying.

  • Every Spotlight is already a real, indexable page.
  • Two ways to drive traffic to them — social, and editorial.

11 — Question for the Chamber

Question — raised by a member

American Nation Bank shows up six times. Is that six memberships?

Ryan at 1NB flagged it — and the pattern isn't unique to them. Six organizations account for 25 separate listings in the roster, because each branch is its own line.

American Nation Bank6 listings First National Bank & Trust of Ardmore6 listings Citizens Bank & Trust Company4 listings Edward Jones4 listings BancFirst3 listings First Bank & Trust Co2 listings

If each branch is a paid membership, the listings are correct as-is. If it's one membership with locations, we should group them under a single Spotlight with its branches listed — cleaner in search, and fairer to everyone else.

Either way it's your call, not a technical one. Tell us which and we'll match the directory to it.
