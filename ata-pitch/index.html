Where Things Stand
Ardmore has real assets.
Most visitors never find them.

Hardy Murphy. Historic downtown. Lake Murray. Antique shopping. Great restaurants. An Amtrak stop. A growing events calendar. The ingredients for a compelling destination are already here — but there isn't a clear system for reaching the right people, telling the right story, and turning a first-time visitor into someone who comes back.

03
What We Talked Through
A few things that
came up in conversation.
  • Visitors who come for Lake Murray or stay the night at a hotel on I-35 often don't make it downtown — there's no clear pull or incentive to explore further.
  • The ExploreArdmore.com calendar is underused. Most people find out about events through Facebook — or they don't find out at all.
  • A discount card program is in development — a smart idea with real potential, but the full value of that system depends on the infrastructure built around it.
  • There's no proactive email or text list for visitors — the ATA pushes information out when it can, but isn't building an audience it owns.
  • And there's no CRM — no central platform to manage that list, automate follow-ups, or keep visitors engaged after they leave town.
  • Success is measured by occupancy and motel tax revenue, but there's no dashboard tracking the marketing metrics that drive those numbers.

Initiative 01 — Flagship
The discount card is a great start.
Let's build the system around it.

The QR-gated discount card is genuinely smart. A page only accessible by card — updatable in real time, no reprinting required. We want to extend that idea into a full visitor communication loop. Instead of scan → discounts, it becomes scan → email capture → discounts → ongoing relationship.

By adding a "Claim this offer" button to each discount, the ATA can see which offers are actually being redeemed — giving you real data on what's driving foot traffic, and giving participating businesses proof that the program works.

01

02

03
Email Capture
Name + email required to unlock the page — low friction, high value exchange
04
"Claim this Offer" Tracking
Each discount has a claim button — the ATA sees what's being redeemed and where

Ongoing Nurture
ATA owns the relationship — events, deals, return-visit incentives via automated email

Initiative 02
Get events in front of
people before they
decide where to go.

Right now Ardmore's event calendar is reactive — people find events if they're already looking. We flip that. A bi-weekly email goes out to the list: "Here's what's happening in Ardmore." The list grows with every QR scan. And as that list grows, it becomes something more — a direct channel to a captive audience of people who've already visited Ardmore and want to come back.

Sent bi-weekly to start — and as the list grows, it becomes valuable promotional space for local businesses and Chamber members to be featured.

Every bi-weekly send reaches people who've already been to Ardmore — the highest-intent audience you have. Over time, that list becomes a media channel the ATA fully owns.

ATA staff submits events via a simple intake form. Hallpass packages approved events into the bi-weekly email — formatted, designed, and distributed automatically.

Initiative 03
Put Ardmore businesses
in the spotlight.

We're already producing social media video content for Ardmore businesses as part of our work with the ADA — POV-style, native-format videos that show real places the way real visitors experience them. These videos are working. The Hamburger Inn video alone pulled 3.5K likes, 230 comments, and 385 shares.

A dedicated ATA budget expands that capacity — more shoots, more businesses, more content showing visitors why Ardmore is worth the trip. Chamber member businesses are the natural priority.

  • Format: Short-form vertical video — authentic, customer-perspective content designed for social media, not a staged business profile
  • Distribution: ATA social channels, business pages, and where appropriate, the visitor discount page itself
  • Synergy: Chamber member businesses get visibility; the ATA gets content that makes Ardmore look like a destination worth exploring
