What we heard

  • A new hire's first day is an eight-hour safety slideshow that half the room sleeps through — and a desk someone else just cleaned out. The first impression is an accident, not a design.

The series

Five short films. Two to five minutes each — punchy, not padded.

I

The History

Where Atlas came from — and where the Ardmore plant fits in it. Big Chief to Georgia Pacific to Atlas; six decades on this ground; "the little shingle plant that could." A new hire learns on day one that they're joining something with roots.

Documentary Archival photos & leadership voices · 2–5 min
II

The Process

Granules in, shingles out — and where your hands fit in the whole. It follows the line from glass mat and coating through cutting and packaging to the distributor, so every new hire understands their part is contributing to something bigger.

Live action · Drone · Animation Aerials for scale, custom animation for the flow · 2–5 min
III

Safety

Brief, but serious: "we send you home the same way you got here — if not better." Lockout / tagout, pinch points, heights, confined space — not the details, but the promise that Atlas invests in protecting you, because a respectful organization starts there.

Documentary On-floor · sets up the full safety orientation · 2–5 min
IV

The Atlas Promise

What Atlas commits to you: a safe floor, fair pay that respects the work, and a real life outside of it — time for your family, and a clean, positive place to spend your day. The promise that turned this plant around, said out loud.

Documentary Leadership & employee voices · 2–5 min
V

The Turnaround

How far this plant has come. Records broken, the cleanest shingle plant in the whole of Atlas, downtime cut by more than half, a floor that respects its people. Told with pride — so a new hire knows, on their first day, that they're joining a plant that's winning.

Documentary KPIs & the people behind them · 2–5 min

The approach

Documentary — not a training video.

The moment a new hire hears "training video," they brace for stilted performances and eighties stock music. We go the other way: real people, the real floor, shot with care and cut for energy. It's the difference between something people endure and something they remember — and because it's built on what's genuinely true here, it earns their trust instead of testing their patience.

Shot well and grounded in what's real, these films don't go stale. This isn't a reel you re-do every time something changes — it's an asset the Ardmore plant welcomes people with for years.
